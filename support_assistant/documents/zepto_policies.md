# Zepto Customer Support & Refund Policies

## Refund Policy
Orders with missing items are eligible for an instant refund to the original payment method within 24 hours of delivery. Photo proof is required for damaged perishables.

## Delivery Time Guarantees
Zepto aims to deliver orders within 10 to 15 minutes. If delivery exceeds 30 minutes due to unannounced delays, customers receive a ₹50 compensation coupon.
